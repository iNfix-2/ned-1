Programs

One League offers a Master of Business Administration (MBA) - along with programs in Generative AI & Data Science and Entrepreneurial Innovation - with curriculum from the world’s leading universities. Classes are conducted in live, online sessions, led by some of the most renowned and accomplished faculty members in the world.

Master of Business Administration
(MBA)

Complete the Business Management Fellowship, serving as the core curriculum for the MBA program, and choose between the Generative AI & Data Science Fellowship or Entrepreneurial Innovation Fellowship to receive a graduate-level Master of Business Administration (MBA) degree from One League.


Fellowships may be completed in any order preferred.

Distinguished & acclaimed

Faculty

Rob Austin

Professor Austin earned his Doctorate in Management and Decision Sciences from Carnegie Mellon University, where his dissertation received the Herbert A. Simon Award. He has been a Professor of Technology and Operations Management at the Harvard Business School for 12 years and of Innovation and Digital Transformation at Copenhagen Business School for 9 years. He has published widely in Harvard Business Review, Information Systems Research, MIT Sloan Management Review, Organization Science, Organization Studies, and the Wall Street Journal. He also is the author of over 10 books and more than 100 published cases and notes. He is currently a professor of Information Systems at Ivey Business School and an affiliated faculty member at Harvard Medical School.

June Cotte

Professor Cotte has a PhD in Marketing and over 25 years of experience teaching Marketing at the undergraduate, MBA, PhD, and executive levels. She is the Editor of the Journal of Consumer Research and on the editorial boards of the Journal of Consumer Psychology and the Journal of Marketing. She is the Co-Founder of “Designing Your Career in the Marketing Academy” for young female marketing scholars. Her work has appeared in the Journal of Consumer Research, Journal of Consumer Psychology, MIT/Sloan Management Review, and the Wall Street Journal, amongst others. She is a Professor of Marketing at the Ivey School of Business and has taught at the Duke Fuqua School of Business across the world.

Noel Maurer

Professor Maurer has a B.A. and PhD from Stanford University. He has worked at Harvard Business School in the BGIE unit for over a decade. Before that, he was a professor at ITAM, a university in Mexico City, during which he co-founded an NGO dedicated to helping small rural communities in Chiapas find new business opportunities. Professor Maurer’s research revolves around two different axes: (1) how companies overcome the barriers to commercializing renewable energy; and (2) how private actors defend their property rights under dictatorial governments or political instability. Professor Maurer is presently an Associate Professor of International Affairs and International Business at the George Washington University.

Lauren Taylor

Professor Taylor has a doctorate from Harvard Business School, a Masters of Divinity as a Presidential Scholar from Harvard’s Divinity School, and a Masters in Public Health and Bachelors in the History of Medicine from Yale University. Professor Taylor researches management, governance and ethics, with a focus on multi-stakeholder coalitions. She has worked as a consultant for the Global Fund and Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation and written on the institutionalization of global health and how to reform the World Health Organization. Her path-breaking book, The American Health Care Paradox, is required reading at medical and public health schools across the nation. Professor Taylor taught professional responsibility at New York University Stern and global health ethics at Harvard Medical School.

Gautam Mukunda

Professor Mukunda has a PhD from Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) and a B.A. magna cum laude from Harvard College. Professor Mukunda is the author of “Indispensable: When Leaders Really Matter” (Harvard Business Review, 2012) and “Picking Presidents: How To Make The Most Important Decision in the World” (University of California Press, 2022). Formerly he was an Assistant Professor at Harvard Business School, Distinguished Visiting Professor at Schwarzman College, Tsinghua University, and a consultant with McKinsey & Company. He is the host of the Nasdaq podcast “World Reimagined with Gautam Mukunda” and the author of numerous articles on leadership, innovation, and policy issues. Professor Mukunda is presently a Research Fellow at Harvard Kennedy School.

Colette Southam

Professor Southam has a Doctor of Philosophy in Finance from Ivey Business School, Canada and more than 20 years of experience teaching case-based finance courses at the undergraduate, MBA, EMBA, and PhD levels. She founded the Australian Business Case Network and is the recipient of Universities Australia Citation for Outstanding Contribution to Student Learning and the Bond University Vice Chancellor’s Award for Outstanding Contribution to Student Learning through Sustained Innovation. Professor Southam is the author of 40 cases and notes, including “Best-sellers” and “Classics” at Harvard, Ivey, and the Case Centre. Her research focuses on helping companies improve their corporate and international financial decision making with specific interests in corporate financial decision making, ESG, carbon market efficiency and international capital raising.

J. Peter Scoblic

Professor Scoblic has a doctorate from Harvard Business School and a bachelor’s degree with honors from Brown University. He is a Senior Fellow with the International Security Program at New America, a Fellow at Harvard’s Kennedy School, and Co-Founder of Event Horizon Strategies. He has published widely on strategy and uncertainty, including in The New York Times, The Washington Post, Science, Harvard Business Review, and Foreign Affairs. He served as Executive Editor of The New Republic and Foreign Policy, and he was Deputy Staff Director of the Senate Committee on Foreign Relations. Professor Scoblic is the author of U.S. vs. Them, published by Viking Press. He teaches Behavioral Decision Making in Harvard’s Professional Development Program.

Ken Merchant

Professor Merchant holds a PhD in Business and Accounting from the University of California, Berkeley, and an MBA from Columbia University. His research specialties include management accounting, management control systems, corporate governance, and ethics. He has authored 11 books, more than 80 articles, nearly 200 teaching cases, and given research presentations in 31 countries. His work has received numerous awards, including five Notable Contribution Awards from the AAA. He served as Senior Associate Dean–Corporate Programs in University of Southern California’s Marshall School of Business and as Dean of USC’s Leventhal School of Accounting. Professor Merchant has over 40 years of teaching experience, including 12 years at Harvard Business School.

About Us

One League is a team of highly dedicated, action-oriented idealists, fighting to democratize access to power, promise, and privilege.

Umaimah Mendhro

Founder & President

Umaimah Mendhro, Founder & President of One League, grew up without access to formal education for much of her life in rural Pakistan and Saudi Arabia, where she lived in exile. Umaimah went on to attend Cornell University and Harvard Business School, graduating with highest distinction (Baker Scholar, top 5% of class). Umaimah is also the Founder of VIDA & Co., formerly backed by Google Ventures and Y Combinator, currently a multi-million dollar public benefit corporation.

Umaimah has worked at Microsoft, McKinsey, and West where she led projects with Twitter, Dropbox, and Square. Umaimah is a Venture Partner with Halogen VC, author of select Harvard Business Review case studies and Forbes Op-Ed pieces, and holds 13 patent awards. Under Umaimah’s leadership, VIDA has been recognized by Fast Company for the World Changing Ideas Award and One League has been recognized as a Fast Company Top 10 Most Innovative Companies in Education in the World. Additionally, Umaimah has been recognized by Inc. as a Female Founders 250 Honoree and by CIO Magazine as one of the Most High-Impact Leaders Making a Difference in the World.

One League is a California based Public Benefit Corporation, approved to operate by the California Bureau for Private Postsecondary Education.
