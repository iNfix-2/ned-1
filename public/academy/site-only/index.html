Apply Now — Structured Intake Process

Getting into NATI is the first step toward an elite aviation career. We utilize a structured intake process to ensure every candidate is ready for the rigors of our flight programs.

Follow the four-step admissions procedure below to initiate your enrollment:

Step 01: Application & Inquiry
Candidates begin by submitting an application detailing their background, objectives, and preferred training track (e.g., Law Enforcement, Corporate Security, or Civilian Enterprise).
Step 02: Aptitude & Baseline Screening
Our admissions team reviews applications and conducts a brief interview to gauge technical aptitude, situational awareness, and commitment to aviation safety standards.
Step 03: Clearance & Documentation
Because our training involves critical airspace operations, candidates must complete standard background checks and submit basic aviation medical fitness confirmations.
Step 04: Enrollment & Pre-Flight Briefing
Accepted candidates receive their comprehensive NATI syllabus, uniform requirements, and ground school preparation materials ahead of their day-one orientation.

Discover NATI

As a premier aviation training institution headquartered in Nigeria, Nethawk Aviation Training Institute (NATI) bridges the gap between traditional manned aviation and advanced Unmanned Aircraft Systems (UAS) training.

NATI Training Centre

Committed to excellence, innovation, and safety, NATI delivers world-class aviation education that aligns with internationally recognised aerospace standards while addressing Africa's evolving aviation and security needs.

Our programmes are designed to equip the next generation of aviation professionals with the technical expertise, operational competence, and leadership required to excel in both defence and commercial aviation. NATI provides specialised training for personnel from the Nigerian Armed Forces, intelligence, security, and law enforcement agencies, while also preparing enterprise UAS pilots to develop proficiency in diverse aviation career paths including critical infrastructure protection, oil and gas operations, aerial surveying, mapping, inspection, environmental monitoring, and other high-value commercial applications.

With a strong emphasis on practical training, regulatory compliance, operational safety, and technological innovation, NATI serves as a centre of excellence for aviation education in Africa.
